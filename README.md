An interactive sales revenue dashboard built in Microsoft Excel
analyzing 1,000 global sales records across 7 regions and 100+ countries.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- **Total Revenue by Year** — Revenue trend from 2010 to 2017
- **Revenue by Year & Region** — Multi-region line chart comparison
- **Revenue by Total Cost by Region** — Bar chart across 7 regions
- **Revenue by Order Priority** — Pie chart breakdown
- **Revenue by Items** — Horizontal bar chart by product category
- **Revenue by Countries** — Country-level revenue comparison

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters (Slicers)

- 🌍 Country
- 📦 Item Type
- 🗺️ Region
- 🛒 Sales Channel (Offline / Online)

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- Revenue **peaked in 2015** at $195M and declined to $96M by 2017
- **North America & Sub-Saharan Africa** led regional revenue
- **Office Supplies** was the top performing category at $289M
- **Household & Cosmetics** followed as 2nd and 3rd top categories
